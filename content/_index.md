I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Economics and Business at Universitat Pompeu Fabra and a Research Affiliate at ESSEC Business School and TILEC (Tilburg University). I received my Ph.D. in Economics from Tilburg University in 2023.

My research is in **empirical industrial organization**, with a focus on market design and competition policy. In my work,  I develop and estimate economic models of strategic interaction, combining structural modeling and econometric methods with detailed market data to quantify welfare effects and assess alternative market designs and policies.

**I am on the 2026–2027 academic job market.**
